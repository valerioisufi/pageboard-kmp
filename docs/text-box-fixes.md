# Text boxes: large boxes, pasting, flowing across pages

Three problems of the text boxes reported from real use: a box larger than the window, text pasted
from other applications, a text going on to a new page. An external report
(`report_problemi_text_box.md`, generated by another assistant) described them with their causes;
this document checks it against the code, and gives the requirements, the design and the plan. It
starts from main at d4b0c35e, 2026-11-04.

The responsibilities are assigned following the GRASP principles (Information Expert, Creator,
Controller, Low Coupling, High Cohesion, Polymorphism, Pure Fabrication, Indirection, Protected
Variations).

## Starting point

Everything marked "measured" was run on the JVM on `EditorScreen` in a window of 1200 × 900, with
injected mouse and touch events and a real Ctrl+V on a clipboard of the test. Nothing was run on
Android or iOS. "Read" means read in the code only.

### Large boxes

1. **The field is smaller than its box: the root cause, not in the report.** `TextBoxEditor` sizes
   the field with `.width(widthDp)` and no height, inside a parent as large as the canvas, so its
   constraints cap it. Measured at 250%, for a box of 188 mm (1969 px):
   * the field is 1200 × 836 px, the frame 1981 × 2928 px;
   * **the lines wrap on the width of the window while written**: 278 mm of height written,
     185 mm once saved. The text changes shape when the editor closes;
   * **the field scrolls inside itself** when its text is taller than it (a box of 896 px at 100% is
     enough): the lines are no longer where the page has them, and are cut at the edge of the field.
2. **The frame moving the box.** `CanvasGestureHandler` starts `EDITOR_MOVE` in the frame
   (line 383), but the field is above the canvas and takes what falls on it. Measured: a mouse drag
   in the field selects text, on the padding of the frame it moves the box. The move the report saw
   (15 → −4 mm) happens where the frame goes on past the capped field: a consequence of 1.
3. **A drag outside the frame already moves the canvas.** Measured: the camera moves, the editor stays
   open (`EDITOR_TAP` becomes `PAN` after the slop, lines 662-672; it closes on a tap only).
4. **Touch on the field does nothing.** Measured: one finger dragged on the field moves nothing;
   a pinch with both fingers on it does not zoom, and moves the caret. A box filling the screen
   leaves no place to pan or zoom with the fingers. Not in the report.
5. **The automatic scroll follows the wrong thing.** `TextEditorOverlay` keeps the bottom of the
   **last line** above the bar, vertically only. Measured: on the first line of a box taller than
   the window the caret stays above the screen while typing. Nothing follows it horizontally. The
   report says no scroll exists; one does.
6. **The handles of the sides** are at the middle of the height of the box (`SelectionFrame.of`),
   off the screen in a tall box even when their edge is visible (read, seen in the screenshots).

### Pasting

7. **In the field the text arrives as it is.** Measured with Ctrl+V: `\r\n` stays, `•` stays a
   character. `TextEditorManager.updateValue` sees the paste only as a change of the value.
8. **On the canvas**, `pasteText` under 600 characters adds the text as it is (measured: `\r\n` kept).
   From 600 characters it opens the import sheet, where the cleaning is an option.
9. **Tables.** `TableEditing.keepInCells` returns `null` when a selection is replaced (line 186).
   Measured: Ctrl+V over a selection of a cell writes a `|` and a line break raw, and the table
   breaks; Backspace over a selection across two rows destroys it. In a cell with a collapsed caret
   the `|` is escaped, but a `\r` stays (`Riga \| uno\r Riga due`): the report missed these two.
10. **Rich text** (HTML, RTF) is read as plain text: true, and already "left for later" in
    `docs/text-import.md`.
11. **`TextCleanup.toMarkdown` is not safe for every paste, as the report proposes.** It joins the
    lines of a paragraph and makes a short line before a paragraph a heading: pasting
    `Ciao\n\nCome stai?` gives `## Ciao`; addresses, verses and lines of code would be joined.

### Flowing across pages

12. **The check runs at each change, the split only on overflow**: measured, one flow in eight
    lines written. The report's "at every keystroke" holds for the measuring only.
13. **Each flow ends the session.** `flowChain` calls `resetSession`, `openAt` a new session, and
    `TextEditorOverlay` is keyed on `sessionId`: the field is made again, `onDispose` hides the
    keyboard, `LaunchedEffect` shows it (read; the flicker on a soft keyboard was not observed). The
    undo steps of the field go with it, and a key typed in that frame is dropped (already in "Left
    for later" of `docs/text-boxes.md`).
14. **The jump is not animated.** `openAt` calls `Viewport.showPage`, centering the page at once.
15. **An Enter alone at the bottom of a page makes a page**, with an empty box: measured, this is the
    empty box of the report's figure 6 (its test types a final `\n`).
16. **Emptying a box leaves its traces.** Measured:
    * the last box emptied, Backspace goes back correctly to the box before, but its `next` still
      names the box removed (saved so), its chevron stays, and the new page stays, empty;
    * **a box in the middle emptied breaks the chain**: `finishEditing` removes it without flowing
      (`value.text.isNotBlank() && flowChain`), the box before keeps a link to nothing, and the
      following box is no longer in the chain. Later edits no longer flow into it.
17. **Lines removed pull the next ones back on closing**, as `docs/text-boxes.md` decides, not while
    written. The report's asymmetry is true for the time, not for the result.

### The report's evidence

* Its figure 1 ("the line and the caret are off the screen") shows both on the screen: the text had
  flowed to page 3. Its zoomed screenshots show no text: the box is above the screen.
* Its proposed flow only on closing goes against requirement 14 of `docs/text-boxes.md`, approved by
  the user ("typing at the end of a page goes on in the next box"): see decision 1.
* Its line references are correct. Its tests (`UserReportedIssuesScreenshotTest`,
  `TextBoxToolDeepTest`, untracked) write to a directory of another tool and are not meant to be
  committed: each phase here adds its own tests.

## Requirements

1. **A box looks the same written and saved**: same lines, same place, at every zoom, however large.
2. **The caret is always in sight while typing or moving with the keys**, on both axes, above the bar
   and the keyboard. Panning or zooming by hand is never undone by it.
3. **The fingers move the canvas over the field too**: a drag pans, a pinch zooms; a tap puts the caret,
   a long press selects, the pen and the mouse select as they do.
4. **A box is moved from its frame only**, never by a gesture on its text.
5. **The side handles are in sight** when a part of their edge is.
6. **A paste never breaks a table**: its pipes escaped, its line breaks spaces, whatever was selected;
   no deletion breaks one either.
7. **A pasted text is clean without being rewritten**: line endings and non-breaking spaces always
   normalized; bullets and wrapped lines turned into lists and paragraphs only when the text
   clearly has them; no heading guessed; nothing changed in a block of code, or in a text that is
   already Markdown. One undo takes the paste away. The same on the canvas and in the field.
8. **Flowing does not interrupt the writing**: no keyboard flicker, no key lost, no jump; the view moves
   only as much as the caret needs, animated.
9. **A page is not made for nothing**: blank lines alone at the bottom never start one.
10. **A chain stays whole**: a box emptied anywhere flows the chain, no link to a removed box is
    saved, no chevron is left. The page a chain made and left blank goes away with it, in the same step
    to undo; a page with anything on it stays.
11. **Nothing stored or sent changes**: no migration, no change of the protocol, no stored text
    rewritten. A `\r` or a link to nothing already in a note is left as it is, and read as today.

## Design

### Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Class (module) | GRASP |
|---|---|---|
| The field laid out at the size of the box, out of the constraints of the canvas | `TextBoxEditor` (`drawview-ui`) | Information Expert |
| How much to pan so that a rectangle is in a visible area, on both axes | `CaretReveal` (`drawview-ui`, new, pure) | Pure Fabrication |
| The caret followed: its rectangle from the layout, the room left by the bar and the keyboard | `TextEditorOverlay` (`drawview-ui`) | Controller |
| The handles on the visible part of the edge | `SelectionFrame.ofEditedText` (`drawview-canvas`) | Information Expert |
| The gestures moving the camera given to the canvas from over the field: a finger past the slop, a second finger, a wheel, a trackpad, the middle button | `canvasGesturesOverField` (`drawview-canvas`, new, public), on `CanvasGestureHandler` | Indirection, Low Coupling |
| A paste recognized in a change of the value | `TextEditorManager.updateValue`, as `SelectionInput` and `keepInCells` | Controller |
| A pasted text normalized (pure, the same on every device) | `PastedText` (`drawview-model`, new), sharing the rules of `TextCleanup` | Pure Fabrication, High Cohesion |
| The cells kept whole for a selection replaced or deleted | `TableEditing.keepInCells` (`drawview-canvas`) | Information Expert |
| The box written changing without a new session | `TextEditorManager.flowChain`, `openAt` | Controller, Protected Variations |
| The room needed by a text, its final blank lines out | `TextEditorManager.flowIfOverflowing` | Information Expert |
| A chain flowed when a box of it is emptied, the link read through the chains | `TextEditorManager.finishEditing`, `flowChain`; `SelectionFrame.compute` with `TextChains` | Creator, Information Expert |
| The blank page of a chain removed in the change of the flow | `TextEditorManager.flowChain` with `RemovePage` | Creator |

### The field

The field is measured with `Constraints.fixedWidth(widthPx)` and an unbounded height through
`Modifier.layout`: it never scrolls inside itself, and takes every gesture on the text, so the frame
moves the box from its padding only (requirement 4 follows). To check on the tablet: the size of
the field at a high zoom.

### The caret

`LaunchedEffect` in `TextEditorOverlay` asks `CaretReveal` for the pan that brings the rectangle of
the caret (`getCursorRect`, through the mapping of the presentation; in a table, the cell) into the
canvas less the bar, the keyboard and a margin. It is keyed on the text and the caret, not on the
camera, so it never fights a pan or a pinch; with a selection, only when the text changes (a drag
selecting is not followed). A box wider than the window scrolls sideways.

### Touch over the field

`canvasGesturesOverField`, at the `Initial` pass of the field, lets a tap and a long press go to the
field; a finger past the slop, or a second finger, becomes a pan or a pinch of the canvas
(`CanvasGestureHandler.takeOver`), its fling and its limits the ones of any gesture there. A wheel, a
trackpad and the middle button of a mouse go to the canvas as well: a field as large as its box
would otherwise leave them nowhere to go. A sibling sharing the events is not possible: Compose asks
the deepest node hit, which is inside the field. The function is public (the field is in
`drawview-ui`), the only addition to the API of `drawview-canvas`.

### Pasting

A change inserting more than one character with a line break, or a `\r`, is a paste (an input method
commits no line break): `updateValue` passes it through `PastedText`, in cells through
`keepInCells`, in a block of code through line endings only. `pasteText` uses `PastedText` too.
`SelectionStep` already undoes a value made on top of what the field typed: it serves here.

`PastedText` always normalizes `\r\n`, `\r`, non-breaking spaces. It turns the text into lists and
paragraphs only if it does not look like Markdown and has at least two bullet lines, joining the lines
wrapped under an item; it joins the lines of a paragraph only when they look wrapped (at least three,
of similar length, the last ones not ending a sentence). It never makes a heading: guessing them stays
with the import sheet, where a preview shows them.

### Flowing

A flow moves the text into the box of the caret without a new `sessionId`: the field stays, with its
focus and its keyboard, and takes the new value and box. Its undo steps are cleared at that point (a
step of another box means nothing here; the undo of the document has them). The guard of the stale
field (phase 9 of `docs/text-boxes.md`) must still hold for what is in flight: the test types keys
across a flow. `openAt` stops calling `showPage`: the new box is followed like any caret, animated.

`flowIfOverflowing` measures the text without its final blank lines, so an Enter alone does not flow;
the next character does.

## Decisions

To be taken by the user:

1. **When a text flows.** Proposed: **while written, without interruptions** (requirements 8 and 9), as
   the approved `docs/text-boxes.md` asks, and fixing what makes it uncomfortable. The alternative is
   the report's: **on closing only**, the box going on below its page while written, over the gap
   and the next page, and the text jumping only at Done. It is a smaller change, but it reverses
   requirement 14 of that plan.
2. **Rich text of the clipboard (HTML).** Proposed: **left for later**, in a plan of its own: reading
   it needs each platform's clipboard, and a bounded parser of what comes from outside.

Taken alone, revertible:

3. **No switch to turn flowing off**, as the report asks: the tiles draw only their page, so the lines
   below it would be cut once saved.
4. **No line of the page break while written**, as the report asks: with a flow without jumps it
   should not be needed. Left for later if it still is.
5. **Lines pulled back on closing**, as today, not while written: pulling them back at each key would
   move the boxes after the caret as much as writing does.
6. **A blank page is one without elements**: a page the user made blank on purpose that a chain
   reaches and then leaves goes too; the step can be undone, and it stays in the trash.
7. **A selection across cells replaced** leaves the text in the first cell and empties the others,
   the shape of the table kept.

## Left for later

- The rich text of the clipboard (decision 2), a paste as plain text (Ctrl+Shift+V).
- A drag selecting text following the pointer past the edge of the window.
- Chains broken by the bug of phase 6 in existing notes stay as they are: their boxes are intact, and
  nothing says which box followed which.
- The lines of a chain pulled back while written.
