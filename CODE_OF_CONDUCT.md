# Code of conduct

This project is made by people in their own time, for people who keep their notes in it. Everyone
who takes part, in the issues, the discussions, the pull requests or anywhere else the project is
spoken of, is treated with respect.

## What is expected

* Be kind and patient, with newcomers above all. A question asked twice is answered again.
* Criticise the work, not the person. Say what is wrong and, when you can, how to make it better.
* Accept that others decide differently. The maintainer has the last word on what goes in, and says
  why.
* Write so that a reader whose first language is not English understands you.

## What is not accepted

* Insults, mockery, and remarks about who a person is: their origin, gender, sexuality, religion,
  age, disability, appearance or experience.
* Harassment, in public or in private, and attention that goes on after it was refused.
* Publishing what others gave in private, their personal data above all.
* Sexual language or images, and threats of any kind.

## What happens

Report what you saw to the maintainer, at pencilnotes@proton.me. A report is read within a week and
kept private; who reported it is not told to anyone else.

Depending on what happened, the maintainer asks the person to stop, removes what they wrote, or
bans them from the project, for a time or for good. The decision is told to the person, with the
reason.

This code applies to every space of the project, and to anyone who speaks for it elsewhere.
